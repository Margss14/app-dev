# Favorite Movies
-list of my most liked movies and series-

1. **Elemental**

Elemental is a 2023 animated movie by Pixar that takes place in Element City, where fire, water, air, and earth residents live together. The story follows Ember, a fiery young woman, and Wade, a water guy, who form an unlikely friendship that challenges their differences. As they get to know each other, they learn about acceptance, love, and finding where they truly belong. The film explores themes of diversity, cultural identity, and emotional connection. With stunning animation and heartfelt storytelling, Elemental shows that even opposites can create something beautiful together.

2. **The Owl House**

The Owl House is an animated fantasy series by Disney about a teenage girl named Luz who accidentally stumbles into a magical world. There, she befriends Eda, a rebellious witch, and King, a tiny demon with big dreams. Despite having no magical powers, Luz decides to become a witch’s apprentice and learns magic in her own creative way. The show explores themes of friendship, identity, and self-acceptance. With its imaginative world and emotional storytelling, The Owl House celebrates being different and finding where you truly belong.

3. **The Dragon Prince**

The Dragon Prince is an animated fantasy series set in the magical land of Xadia, where humans and elves are divided by ancient conflict. The story follows two human princes, Callum and Ezran, and an elf assassin named Rayla as they embark on a journey to return a dragon egg and restore peace. Along the way, they face dangerous enemies, powerful magic, and deep moral choices. The series explores themes of unity, forgiveness, and the power of friendship. With its rich world-building and heartfelt story, The Dragon Prince shows that peace can be achieved when people choose understanding over hate.
